India's Economic Transformation Dashboard
An interactive data visualization dashboard exploring India's remarkable economic progress from 2011-2025, based on insights from leading economists Dr. Karthik Muralidharan and Dr. Shamika Ravi.

Overview
Features
Interactive charts and visualizations
Dark mode support
Downloadable datasets (CSV format)
State-level poverty comparisons
Research study summaries with ROI analysis
Mobile-responsive design


This dashboard presents research-backed data on:

Poverty Reduction: India's poverty rate declined from 29.5% (2011-12) to 4.9% (2023-24)
Governance Efficiency: Evidence showing 10-100x ROI from better governance vs. increased spending
Consumption Growth: Rural-urban convergence and dietary diversification across income groups
Digital Transformation: UPI's exponential growth to 18.39 billion transactions per month
Economic Indicators: GDP growth, inflation trends, and development metrics.

Data Sources
All data is sourced from official publications and peer-reviewed research:

Ministry of Statistics (MoSPI): Household Consumption Expenditure Surveys, GDP data
NITI Aayog: National Multidimensional Poverty Index
Reserve Bank of India: Updated poverty estimates
National Payment Corporation of India: UPI transaction statistics
Academic Research: Studies published in American Economic Review, Econometrica, and other journals

Key Research Papers
Muralidharan, K., Niehaus, P., & Sukhtankar, S. (2016). "Building State Capacity: Evidence from Biometric Smartcards in India." American Economic Review
